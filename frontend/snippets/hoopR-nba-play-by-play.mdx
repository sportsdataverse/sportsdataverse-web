hoopR ships season-level NBA (and men's college basketball) play-by-play as
prebuilt releases. Loading a season is two lines; from there it's ordinary
dplyr.

<CodeTitle title="nba_pbp.R" lang="r" />

```r
# install.packages("hoopR")
library(hoopR)
library(dplyr)

pbp <- load_nba_pbp(seasons = 2025)

# What does the league's shot diet look like?
pbp |>
  filter(shooting_play) |>
  count(type_text, sort = TRUE) |>
  head(10)
```

Every shooting play carries `coordinate_x` / `coordinate_y`, so this is also
the raw material for shot charts. For college hoops, swap in
`load_mbb_pbp()` — same shape, same columns.

Full docs: [hoopR.sportsdataverse.org](https://hoopR.sportsdataverse.org/)

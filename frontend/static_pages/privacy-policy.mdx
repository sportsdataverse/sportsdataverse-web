At [sportsdataverse.org](https://sportsdataverse.org), we value your privacy and take it seriously. We collect personal information only when you give it to us on purpose — today that means the email address you enter in the newsletter sign-up. Browsing the site does not require an account and we do not collect names or contact details otherwise.

**Newsletter.** When you subscribe, we store your email address in our own database and pass it to [Resend](https://resend.com), the service that delivers our email, so we can send you the newsletter. We use it for nothing else. To be removed, use the unsubscribe link in any email or write to [sportsdataverse@gmail.com](mailto:sportsdataverse@gmail.com) and we will delete your record.

**Survey and join.** If you answer our questionnaire we store your answers (what you do, which languages, sports, and packages you use, how you found us, and how you'd like to hear from us). The anonymous survey stores no email or name. On the join form we also store the email and name you give us; if you ask for the newsletter we send a confirmation link first and add you to our email list only when you use it. These answers are used to decide what to build and where to post, in aggregate.

**Analytics.** We use [Plausible](https://plausible.io), a cookie-less analytics service, to count page views and clicks on our own links (for example which social or donation link was used, and from where on the page). Plausible does not collect personal data or track you across sites; the counts are not linked to any specific user and are used for internal purposes only. We also keep a visitor count for blog posts in our own database, likewise not linked to anyone.

We do not share any information collected through this website with any third parties other than the email delivery and analytics services named above, except as required by law.

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time, and will notify you of any changes by posting the updated policy.

If you have any questions or concerns about the Privacy Policy, please contact us at [sportsdataverse@gmail.com](mailto:sportsdataverse@gmail.com).

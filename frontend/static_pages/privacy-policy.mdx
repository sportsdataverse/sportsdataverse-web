At [sportsdataverse.org](https://sportsdataverse.org), we value your privacy and take it seriously. We collect personal information only when you give it to us on purpose — today that means what you enter in the newsletter sign-up, the join form or the survey — your name, email and where you're based, plus a mailing address if you ask for stickers. Browsing the site does not require an account and we do not collect names or contact details otherwise.

**Newsletter.** When you subscribe, we store your email address in our own database and pass it to [Resend](https://resend.com), the service that delivers our email, so we can send you the newsletter. If you subscribed through the join form, we also pass the profile answers you most recently gave us on the join form or the survey — your role, the languages and sports you use, how you found us and how you'd like to hear from us — so we can send you the parts of the newsletter that are relevant to you instead of all of it. We use this for nothing else. To be removed, use the unsubscribe link in any email or write to [sportsdataverse@gmail.com](mailto:sportsdataverse@gmail.com) and we will delete your record.

**Survey and join.** If you answer our questionnaire we store your answers (what you do, which languages, sports, and packages you use, how you found us, and how you'd like to hear from us); in aggregate, these help us decide what to build and where to post. Both the survey and the join form also ask for your name, your email, and where you're based — your country, and your state or province if you're in the US, Canada or Australia; city is optional — plus, if you choose to add them, your social media handles and your affiliations (your team, league, outlet, company or university). Answers given through the survey before September 25, 2026 were anonymous — no email or name — and stay that way. Your location tells us where the community is; we never use it to mail you anything. Giving us your email means we may contact you about SportsDataverse collaborations, research, or the answers you gave — write to [sportsdataverse@gmail.com](mailto:sportsdataverse@gmail.com) and we'll stop. On the join form, if you ask for the newsletter we may send you a confirmation link first; where we do, you are added to our email list only when you use it. Everything you submit is stored in our database, which SportsDataverse's maintainers can access directly to run the site. SportsDataverse admins can also search and filter everyone who has filled in the survey or the join form — seeing each person's name, email, role, country and affiliation together in a list — and can open any one person to see all of their answers and past submissions. Contact lists built from that list for outreach include only people who have filled in the survey or the join form since September 25, 2026, when we started showing this consent notice next to the email field; anyone who submitted before then, who has asked us not to contact them, or who has unsubscribed from the newsletter, is left out of every such list, and we keep a record each time one is built. SportsDataverse org members reviewing a Discord request can see that requester's self-reported affiliations and social handles, but not their location or their other answers, and only while the request is awaiting a decision.

**Package submissions.** If you submit a package through the join form, we store what you told us about it (its name, links, and description). It is not shown on the public packages page until a SportsDataverse member reviews it and approves it; until then it is visible only in our internal package-review tool.

**Stickers.** If you ask for stickers on the join form, we store your name and mailing address so we can send them. Your address is kept apart from everything else we store about you, and it is readable only by a SportsDataverse org admin, on an internal admin-only page — it is never included in any email we send you, and it is never passed to Resend or any other outside service. The address is removed as soon as we mail your stickers, or if the request is cancelled before then; after we mail them, we keep your name, the date we mailed them, and which admin mailed them, linked to your record, as a record of what we sent.

**Discord.** If you ask for a Discord invite, we record that request together with the resulting decision (admitted immediately, or approved/declined by a SportsDataverse org member) and, if you were signed in with GitHub to ask, your GitHub username, so we can see who vouched for you. We ask Discord to create your invite — a private link good for a handful of uses over 7 days, never posted publicly — but we do not send Discord your email address or answers. If our own records already vouch for you (an existing org member, or someone with a merged pull request in the `sportsdataverse` GitHub organization), the invite is returned to you immediately; otherwise a member of the SportsDataverse GitHub organization reviews it by hand in our internal review tool. Any member of that organization can open the tool, and it shows your name, your email address, what you asked for, the request's status, who reviewed it and any reason given, and the GitHub username you were signed in with, if any. In that tool — the one every SportsDataverse org member can open — your survey answers are never shown person by person; they are combined into totals. That limit is about that member tool only: as described above, a SportsDataverse admin can still look at one person's individual answers. Deleting a record entirely is limited to organization admins. Once we can send mail from a verified address, an admitted invite is also emailed to you through Resend, which then receives your email address for that message; until then, an invite approved from the review queue is sent to you by hand.

You can ask us to delete your record at any time — write to [sportsdataverse@gmail.com](mailto:sportsdataverse@gmail.com) and we will remove it from our database, along with every survey or join submission linked to your email (your answers and their history — an answer given anonymously, with no email attached, can't be linked to you and so can't be found or deleted this way), any package you submitted that has not been published yet, and any sticker request on file for you, mailed or not. A Resend contact created from a newsletter sign-up is removed separately, and deleting your record does not revoke an invite already sent to you.

**Analytics.** We use [Plausible](https://plausible.io), a cookie-less analytics service, to count page views and clicks on our own links (for example which social or donation link was used, and from where on the page). Plausible does not collect personal data or track you across sites; the counts are not linked to any specific user and are used for internal purposes only. We also keep a visitor count for blog posts in our own database, likewise not linked to anyone.

We do not share any information collected through this website with any third parties other than the email delivery, Discord, and analytics services named above, except as required by law.

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time, and will notify you of any changes by posting the updated policy.

If you have any questions or concerns about the Privacy Policy, please contact us at [sportsdataverse@gmail.com](mailto:sportsdataverse@gmail.com).
